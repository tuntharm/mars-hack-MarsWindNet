Briefing · not the live city demo

MarsWindNet ML plots

Walk-through of the four-node synthetic nowcast. This page is a static gallery. The main settlement viewer is unchanged.

Filled colour maps are the 25×25 simulator, same idea as github.com/tuntharm/mars-hack-MarsWindNet/tree/main/mars_simulation_plots. XGBoost predicts only city-centre u, v, dust — not that grid. Provenance: CFD/analysis.py synthetic series, not CFD, not the 128×128 city layout.

01 · say this first

What a real field looks like

These three frames are the reduced-order 2 km / 25×25 run: west-to-east wind, dust pooling away from the fast side. This is the picture people expect from GitHub.

02 · the CSV

What we actually trained on

The training file is not those 625 cells. It is four perimeter nodes plus one city sample, once per second. All four nodes see almost the same large-scale wind and dust.

03 · model output

What XGBoost actually emits

Three numbers per timestep: city_u, city_v, city_dust. On the held-out end of the file the u and dust models sit on a train-mean line (RMSE u 1.21, v 0.44, dust 0.44).

04 · why the first maps looked empty

Five samples are not a contour

If you only colour the cells the CSV contains, you get four nodes and a city star. The grey is not a failed contour — those cells were never in the model.

05 · show this last

Simulator field versus interpolated ML city

Left: true 25×25 field. Right: inverse-distance fill from four CSV nodes plus the ML city. The dark blob at CITY is the weak nowcast. Retrain can replace that blob; it cannot be claimed as an ML wind map until the model predicts the grid.
